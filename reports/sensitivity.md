# Simulator sensitivity (Morris elementary effects)

Generated by `python -m packr.sim.sensitivity`. Sample: 300 real train rows per category (equal per commodity), 64 Monte Carlo trials per row, 40 Morris trajectories, 8 levels, common random numbers.

`mu*` = mean absolute elementary effect when the factor moves across its whole range: the change in mean success rate (0 to 1) or in mean ln(shelf-life p50). `sigma` large relative to `mu*` means non-linear or interacting. **Ranges drive the ranking**: assumed scale parameters x0.5 to x2, bounded ones additive (column `range`). Composition (moisture, fat) comes from USDA FDC and is varied only +-20 %, so it is shown for context and marked *sourced*.

This ranking is the order for literature look-ups (spec section 11). It says nothing about which values are right.

Coupled factors: `Constants.d_o2_air` / `Constants.film_hole_cm` and `Constants.d_wv_air` / `Constants.seal_channel_cm` enter the physics only as diffusion-coefficient-over-path-length ratios (hole conductance), so each pair is one quantity to look up.
